About

Software should feel simple, even when it isn't.

My foundation in programming started back in high school at O.P. Jindal School. What began as learning Informatics Practices quickly turned into a genuine interest in building tools that actually solve problems.

Before heading to IILM University, I took an intentional gap year. That time gave me the space to reform my character, figure out what I actually want to build, and double down on self-directed learning. Now, I lean on a mix of foundational coding and AI-assisted engineering to bring ideas like desktop utilities and automation scripts to life.

"If it's annoying to do by hand, I'll probably try to automate it."

When I'm not writing code, you can usually find me playing my acoustic guitar late at night, or geeking out over mechanical keyboards and high-fidelity audio gear.
